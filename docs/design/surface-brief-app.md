Scope: the whole Quizly app (Operate mode): home, set editor, set page, flashcards, Learn, settings.
Audience and job: one owner learning English vocabulary on an Android phone in short sessions.
Constraints: offline PWA, one-handed use, English UI, pronunciation via device voices.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Quizlet's study loop, played straight and without a paywall; the app disappears and leaves the word, its sound and the next step. Refuses novel layouts the owner would have to relearn.
OWN-WORLD: cool grey-blue ground (#F6F7FB) or deep navy (#0A092D), white / #2E3856 cards with 16px corners, one blue #4255FF for actions, green / orange / red only for learning state, Figtree, Lucide icons.
STORY: the owner opens the app, continues Learn in one tap, hears the pronunciation, and after each round sees the mastered count grow.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home; Quizly wordmark top left, "Jump back in" card with progress bar and full-width "Continue learning" button in the top third, set list below, bottom bar Home / + / Settings.
FORM: category canon pinned by the user ("make it look like Quizlet"); no concept-seed round; seed key: none.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
