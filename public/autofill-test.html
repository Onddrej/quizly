Autofill bar test

Temporary page. Tap into each field in turn and look at the strip above the keyboard (passwords, payment methods, addresses). Then press Bar shown or No bar under that field. At the end press Copy results and paste the text to Claude.

A. Current Quizly field

type=text, autocomplete=off, inside a form with autocomplete=off.

B. Plain field

type=text with no attributes at all (the baseline).

C. Unrecognised autocomplete word

autocomplete="quizly-answer", name="quizly-answer" (Chrome skips words it does not know).

D. Read-only until tapped

readonly, the attribute is removed on focus.

E. Search field

type=search, autocomplete=off.

F. Text area

One-row textarea, autocomplete=off.

G. Editable block

A div with contenteditable (not a form control at all).

H. One-time-code field

autocomplete="one-time-code".

I. Form with a submit button

A form with a plain text field and a submit button, no autocomplete attributes.

J. Plain text area (as in Journal)

A multi-line textarea with no attributes and no form, like the note field in the Journal app.
